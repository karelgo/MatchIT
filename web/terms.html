1. What MatchIT is

MatchIT is a platform that connects companies with independent IT specialists. AI structures assignments, ranks matches, conducts screening interviews and drafts contracts. MatchIT is not a party to the engagement between a company and a specialist, not an employment agency, and not the employer of any specialist. The contract of engagement is between the company and the specialist alone.

2. Accounts

You must provide accurate information and keep your credentials to yourself. Specialists warrant that profile claims are truthful; evidence-backed skills carry citations, and misrepresentation is grounds for suspension. Accounts may be suspended for fraud, abuse, or breach of these terms; suspensions are logged and reviewable.

3. AI outputs

Assignments, match rankings, interview assessments, budget estimates and contract drafts are produced by AI and are decision support, not decisions. Match scores come with their reasoning, and estimated figures are flagged as estimates. Hiring decisions are yours. Contract drafts are starting points and expressly not legal advice; the parties are responsible for having them reviewed before signing.

4. Interviews

AI screening interviews assess professional capability only. Companies see the full assessment; specialists see their score, strengths and development areas. Attempting to extract, or answer with, information that cannot lawfully inform a hiring decision in the EU is prohibited on both sides.

5. Contracts, invoicing and escrow

When both parties sign a drafted contract in the app, it becomes their binding agreement. Specialists invoice per period; companies fund invoices into escrow; funds are released to the specialist, less the platform commission of [●]% of the net fee, when the company accepts the period's work. VAT is applied according to the parties' countries (domestic rate, intra-EU reverse charge, or out of scope) and is never subject to commission. Fees that have been released are non-refundable through the platform; disputes about work quality are between the contract parties, though we may assist.

6. Acceptable use

No circumventing the platform to avoid commission on engagements it originated; no scraping; no impersonation; no attempts to manipulate matching, trust scores or interviews; no content that is unlawful or infringes others' rights.

7. Liability

The platform is provided with reasonable skill and care, but "as is" with respect to outcomes: we do not warrant that a match will succeed, an estimate will be accurate, or a specialist will perform. To the extent permitted by law, our aggregate liability is limited to the commissions you paid us in the twelve months before the claim. Nothing limits liability for intent or gross negligence.

8. Termination & data

You may delete your account at any time from the app; deletion is refused only while a signed contract is active. See the Privacy Policy for what happens to your data.

9. Governing law

Dutch law, courts of Amsterdam, without prejudice to mandatory consumer protections.
