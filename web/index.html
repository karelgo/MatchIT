No forms

Talk, don't filter

“We need two Microsoft Fabric architects to migrate our warehouse within six months.” That sentence is the whole intake. The concierge asks the two or three questions a good recruiter would, and the assignment converges — with any AI-estimated budget clearly flagged as an estimate.

Explainable

Every rank has a reason

Matches are scored on skills, semantics, rate, availability, location and language — and the breakdown ships with every match. You see why someone is first, not just that they are.

Evidence

Skills with citations

CVs and public GitHub work are read into the skill graph, and every skill carries the project that proves it. The AI interview then probes exactly what the profile leaves unproven.

Teams

Whole teams, seat by seat

Multi-role assignments are filled role by role — nobody is counted twice, and an open seat is reported honestly rather than padded with a near-miss.

Trust

Contract to payment, inside

The engagement contract is drafted from the terms you agreed — never invented — signed in-app, and invoiced through escrow with EU VAT handled correctly.

EU-first

Built for Europe
