Privacy Policy

Draft. This policy describes the product accurately but has not yet been reviewed by counsel and is not in force. It must be reviewed before launch, and the data controller's legal identity and contact details completed.

Last updated: 27 July 2026 · Controller: MatchIT B.V. (in formation), Netherlands

What we collect

Only what the service needs to work:

  • Account — name, email, role, and a password hash (Argon2id) or your Apple ID's stable identifier if you sign in with Apple. We never see your Apple password.
  • Specialist profiles — the skills, rates, availability, languages, location and links you provide; and, if you choose to import them, the text of your CV and an analysis of your public GitHub repositories.
  • Assignments and matches — problem descriptions, the AI-structured assignment, match scores and their reasoning.
  • Interviews, messages and contracts — AI interview transcripts and assessments, chat messages, contract terms and signature timestamps, invoices.
  • Security records — an append-only log of security-relevant actions (sign-ins, exports, deletions) with the IP address they came from.

We deliberately do not collect or extract age, gender, nationality, health or similar characteristics — even when a CV states them — because they cannot lawfully inform a hiring decision and have no place in a skill graph.

How AI processes your data

AI is the core of MatchIT. Profile and assignment content is sent to large-language-model providers (Anthropic and/or OpenAI, configurable per deployment) to structure assignments, rank matches, conduct screening interviews and draft contracts. Before prompting, profiles are projected down to the fields the task needs — the interviewing model, for example, never receives your name, contact details, location or rate. Match rankings and interview scores always come with their reasoning, and AI-estimated figures (such as budgets) are flagged as estimates.

Legal bases

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — running your account, matching, interviews, contracts and payments.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — security logging, rate limiting and fraud prevention.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — importing your CV or GitHub profile, and push notifications; both are opt-in actions you take explicitly.

Sharing

Your profile is shown to companies whose assignments you match, and their assignments to you — that is the service. Interview assessments are shared asymmetrically by design: the hiring manager sees the full assessment including concerns and a recommendation; you see your score, strengths and development areas. Processors: our cloud infrastructure (EU regions only), LLM providers as above, and — when payments launch — Stripe. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

Export and erasure are built into the product, not a support ticket: Download my data returns everything we hold about you as portable JSON (Arts. 15/20), and Delete my account erases it (Art. 17). Erasure is refused only while a signed contract is in force (Art. 17(3)), because an active engagement is a live obligation on both parties; complete or cancel it first. Security-log entries survive erasure with your identifier removed. You can also complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Retention & security

Data is kept while your account exists and deleted with it, except invoices (statutory retention) and the anonymised security log. All data is stored in EU regions, encrypted in transit and at rest. Access tokens live 15 minutes; refresh tokens rotate on every use and are stored only as hashes.
