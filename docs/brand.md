# MatchIT — Brand

## Positioning

**The AI staffing platform.** Not "Uber for IT", not a marketplace, not a job
board: those frames all imply searching through people. MatchIT's frame is that
AI does the recruiting — understanding on both sides, minutes end to end —
and humans make the decision.

One-liner: **Hire IT specialists in minutes, not weeks.**

## Voice

- **Plain over clever.** "Describe your problem" — not "unlock synergies".
- **Show the reasoning.** The product explains every ranking and score; the
  copy does the same. Never "our proprietary algorithm".
- **Honest about AI.** Estimates are labelled estimates. Drafts are labelled
  drafts. The brand never pretends the AI is a person or an oracle.
- **European, not translated-American.** GDPR, reverse-charge VAT and the DBA
  are treated as first-class product facts, not compliance footnotes.

## Design language

Already implemented in `ios/MatchIT/DesignSystem` and `web/index.html`:

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Accent | `#4A54F1` (indigo) | One confident accent; actions, scores, links |
| Accent soft | accent @ 10–16% | Chips, fills, skill-bar tracks |
| Success | `#29A86E` | Mutual matches, signatures, evidence seals |
| Danger | `#DE4252` | Destructive actions, rejections |
| Surfaces | System backgrounds | Calm, light/dark native |
| Type | SF Pro / system, rounded for display | Large cards, generous space |
| Radius | 20pt cards, 14pt buttons | Soft but not bubbly |

Principles: one accent colour, evidence marked with a seal (✓), scores always
shown as rings with the number visible, and no decoration that isn't
information.

## Logo directions (to brief a designer)

1. **The arrow-M** — an "M" whose middle stroke is the loop's forward arrow
   (→); captures "problem → person" in one glyph. Preferred.
2. **Match point** — two rounded rectangles (a company card and a person card)
   overlapping where their corners meet, forming a spark/star in the overlap.
3. **Wordmark-first** — "Match**IT**" with IT in the accent colour (as the
   landing page does today); ship-now option, works everywhere.

Avoid: handshakes, magnifying glasses, robots, brains — the clichés of both
industries this product replaces.

## Naming note

"MatchIT" is a working title. Before launch: trademark search (EUIPO), domain,
and App Store availability check. The brand system above survives a rename.
