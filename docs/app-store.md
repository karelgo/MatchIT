## Description

MatchIT is the AI staffing platform for companies and IT specialists in Europe.

**If you're hiring:** describe your problem the way you'd tell a colleague —
"we need two Microsoft Fabric architects to migrate our warehouse by March."
The AI concierge writes the complete assignment, asks only the questions a good
recruiter would, and shows you ranked specialists with the reasoning behind
every rank. Interview candidates with AI-generated questions that target
exactly what their profile leaves unproven. When you've found your match, the
contract is drafted from the terms you agreed and signed in the app.

**If you're a specialist:** build a profile that proves itself. Import your CV
or your public GitHub work and every skill gets a citation. Swipe through
opportunities matched to what you can actually do, take the AI interview on
your own schedule, and get constructive feedback either way. Contracts and
invoicing — with escrow and EU VAT handled — live where the work starts.

- AI-written assignments from plain language
- Explainable matching: see why every match ranks where it does
- Evidence-backed skill graphs from CV and GitHub import
- AI screening interviews with fair, constructive feedback
- Team building for multi-role assignments
- In-app contracts, signatures, escrow invoicing and EU VAT
- Real-time chat on every mutual match
- GDPR built in: download or delete everything, any time

MatchIT connects the parties; the engagement contract is between company and
specialist. AI outputs support your decision — they don't make it.
